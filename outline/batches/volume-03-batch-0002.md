**The seventh was refused, and the closed canon is the reason.** The review returned that the same service is a **tram** at two points in Chapter 119 and a **bus** at five, and that *guard* and *conductress* are bus vocabulary, so the two *tram* instances are the outliers. It is the other way round. `chapter-0069.md` opens, in a closed volume, with *The tram signal at the Green Span end of the **Northglass line***, prints the certificate as *Signal, Green Span terminus, Northglass line, clock-driven*, and says in the same chapter *The conductor is a competent woman who has kept a timetable for twenty-six years*; the Green Span line is a tram line in Volume 01 and Volume 02 and always has been. **So the service is a tram, the two *bus* instances are the outliers and are now *tram*, the *guard* at a tram stop is now the *conductor*, and *conductress* is now *conductor* in 119 and 120 to match the book's own word for a woman in that job.** The review's diagnosis of the inconsistency was right and its direction was wrong, and five files that had taken *bus* and *conductress* into their summaries have been corrected with it.
