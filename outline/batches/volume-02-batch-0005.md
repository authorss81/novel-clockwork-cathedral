## Batch goal

Run the rehearsal, make the room refuse the number, and leave a district holding a right it can be held to.

1. **It finds what the seven days between the asking and the running are for, and it finds it on the page and not by assertion.** A notice is not a document. A notice has no form. The only instrument in this city by which anybody is told anything before it happens is a printed sheet with a week in it, posted on a ward board, and a ward board in a district of a hundred and forty thousand is read by about eleven people a day. **A rehearsal is not a schedule, so nobody is entitled to post a notice of one and there is no form on which one could be asked for, and a notice of a rehearsal would be a form, and a form is a way of asking without the rule.** The asking is therefore the notice, and the six days from the fourth to the ninth are the making of it on foot, and the seventh day is the tenth, and the rehearsal falls on the seventeenth because the notice runs to the sixteenth and a notice has to be allowed to expire before the thing.
2. **It runs the rehearsal once, in Chapter 95, and it works, and what it establishes is not a number.** Three clocks on one line are the same length of iron seen three times; a proposal can be traced across a district by three people standing in three places; and nobody can be shown where a person's hours are in it. It stops nothing, certifies nothing, has no seal and no form, and the count of keeper's tolls is four and does not move.
3. **It puts the volume's climax in a room rather than on a machine, and the room refuses the number.** A toll of minutes has to name a district; the district it names is four streets from the street the water comes off; the minutes and the water are two places in one sentence. **The work is not refused. The number is.** And the man who put the number on a wall has to rub it off, with the ward clerk, because a figure that a man rubs off on his own is a figure with a name on it, and the whole year has been about a name on a wall.
4. **It delivers all five of `outline/volume-02.md` §Concrete resolution, and answers all six of batch 0004's open questions, and opens two and only two for Volume 03.**

**One thing this file should record because it is the shape the failure takes in this volume. Six of the thirteen above are a plan and a page disagreeing about a number, and in five of those six the page is doing arithmetic and the plan is carrying a figure. The two that are not disagreements are the two that would have been silent: a fixed figure of eleven changed to twelve by a chapter, and a guardrail satisfied by not repeating an error rather than by getting a number right. The house rule this batch follows is that a figure in a prompt is a claim about a page, and a figure on a page is a claim about the world, and the third one is the only one a reader can check.**
