## The batch in one paragraph

A man with a suspended licence walks four and a half miles of branch with two watches and finds a ramp, and the far end of the ramp is a water gate. A competent auditor tells the district, in writing, on two boards, that it is outside the reliable model, and she is right. A twenty-year-old tells the man, in a room of eleven, why his district is outside everything, and is right. A veteran operator tells him that he cannot feel a thing that is not connected, and that everything done to this district for eighteen years has been done by disconnection. A sealed room's index is requested and neither granted nor refused. A warden hands in her post in advance. A class of licence is abolished and a laundry pays sixty-two shillings for the privilege of being counted. A pumping station starts a stage twice and a cellar four streets away fills up. A hospital sister says a number out loud and refuses to let anybody divide it. And on a counter on Cathedral Rise a woman of twenty-six tells a man with a notebook that the thing he paid eighty-two shillings for in May is a return, and a return is not a party to anything, and **a form is not a body** — which is the correction of aim the volume turns on.
