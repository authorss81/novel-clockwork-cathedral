# Bible Index

Canon for *The Clockwork Cathedral*. Read these before drafting or reviewing chapters.

## Precedence, when files disagree

1. `bible/` — the world, the rules, and the people. It is the floor.
2. `state/continuity.md` — canon facts as established on the page, plus the volume index.
3. `outline/series.md` — the fourteen volumes, the final arc, and the distribution of pressure types.
4. `outline/ending.md` — the beat sheet for the last volume. It refines `series.md`; it never contradicts the bible.
5. `outline/volume-01.md` and `outline/batches/` — route within a volume. These are written after the bible and may not invent rules.
6. Prose.

If two files at the same level disagree, the earlier phase is corrected and the note is recorded in `state/continuity.md`. Prose never silently overrides a bible file; a chapter that needs a new rule writes the rule into the bible first.

## Four things every chapter must respect

1. A Reading is a model, not a prophecy, and it always names what it did not represent.
2. Every answer is paid for — in clock reserve, labor, or a person's lived attention. Free forecasts are not allowed.
3. Orrin's private sense is not a Reading. It has no blind margin, cannot be filed, and always costs him. It is a lead, not an answer.
4. Growth changes his options and his costs. It never removes the need for other people's records, consent, and hands.
