## Central mystery, in three layers

1. **Surface:** why does the Cathedral's public clock tick a ninth time, and why does a correct Reading leave a district worse off?
2. **Historical:** what happened during the Long Nine, who authorized the Censor Gate, and what did Edwin Vale do when he opened the bypass?
3. **Structural:** what is the Dark Reservoir, and why does the machine grow more willing to remove dissent as it grows more accurate?

## Intentionally unresolved, and not to be "fixed"

- The original Cooperative engineers' private reasoning.
- The lived intervals taken by severe body tolls. Some families know; some never will.
- Whether the First Hour had anything like awareness. The book confirms a rule and a learned threshold, not a mind. A character may use the word *remember*; the narration may not.
- How much uncertainty Morrowfen can afford. That is a continuing civic question, not a dangling supernatural thread.

## Promises the Volume 01 outline now owes inside its own volume

These are commitments made by `outline/volume-01.md` and `outline/batches/volume-01-batch-0001.md`. They are not mysteries; they are scheduled beats inside Volume 01, and a batch that has not reached one of them has drifted.

- **Imani Rook blocks the public toll ledger demand in Chapter 10 and files it herself about three chapters later (approximately Chapter 13),** having been shown arithmetic rather than sentiment. She must not be softened before that, and she must not be given the volume's villainy. She appears in **three** volumes after this one (03, 09, 13) and is not a series regular; a draft that treats her as one is wrong.
- **Sel Kade's tenant review of the diverted reserve is owed by about Chapter 46.** The review is a process she helps design, which is why she is still a character in Volume 02.
- **A permanent condition in Orrin's right-ear hearing is seeded in Chapter 9** and must be carried through the rest of the series. It is a ring above a given pitch and a dullness when he is tired — **not** the loss of clean single-machine hearing. That is Volume 08's long-range injury and then Volume 14's final cost, and both must still be available when those volumes are written.
- **The Volume 01 suspension is for certifying a local toll he had no licence to certify.** If a later draft suspends him for a different offence, the volume's argument has been lost. He certifies nothing in Chapter 9, and that offence is reserved for Chapters 44–49.
- **Orrin's class-three renewal falls due about eight weeks after Chapter 1** and Hask holds it. The leash is slack in Chapter 10 and is scheduled to be felt in Chapters 11–20.
- **The ninth tick is still unexplained at the end of Chapter 50.** No batch may resolve it.
