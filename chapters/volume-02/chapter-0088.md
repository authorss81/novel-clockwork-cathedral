# Chapter 88 — The Fifteenth of the Tenth, Two Months On

On Tuesday the twenty-second of the eleventh month a woman of thirty-three put a blind margin on a wall in a pumping ward, and it was not a feeling and it was not a mood, it was five sentences with a working on them, and the working is four hundred and eleven numbers and the margin is that a person can be in two of them, and the name of the thing she did about it is not hers, and she is not going to be thanked, and the man in the room with her told her one fact in September and did not tell her another one on Tuesday, and she noticed, and he did not, and she did not ask, and it is the only thing in the afternoon that is not resolved.

---

**Saturday the nineteenth of the eleventh. The fifteenth of the tenth was two months ago this week, and a committee of nine men with a property qualification sat on a Saturday, which it may do about twice a year, and could not produce the winter rotation, and the fact was minuted in the only words minutes in that building have for it.**

What has happened to it since. The copy made at fourpence a page in four hands, with the fifteen minutes on the face of it, is still on the counter of the Water Users' Committee, and the clerk's line is still under it, and the winter rotation is in force, and it is a worse schedule than the standing one, and it is lawful, and it is correct, and the front is short four times a day between one and four in the morning and has been since 1911 and the district cannot be consulted about it. Nobody has asked about the copy. It is a sheet of foolscap on a counter in a room above a shop in Bellweather and it has been there for thirty-three days and four people have read it, and one of them is the chandler, and one of them is the clerk, and the other two are not on the committee and have not been in that room since the Monday morning of the seventeenth of the tenth, which is the morning they made it in and the day the clerk entered it.

**And in the same week a figure arrived that anybody in this city can now use, and a figure is a thing a committee can vote on, and a range was the only safe thing on two boards in the Cathedral Rise in September and is now a courtesy with a figure inside it.**

---

**Tuesday the twenty-second, about half past three in the afternoon, at the Office of Public Reckoning, in a building with a court outside the window, and there were three of the four staff in the room and one of them had been in the room on Friday.**

She had it in a folder. It is four sheets of the Office's own foolscap and it is the whole of the audit and it has been published on a board outside a public Archive for twenty-four days and the queue at that board has fallen from nine a day to two, and nobody has thanked her, and she has told three people in this building that she is not going to be thanked and has said the reason and it is not modesty.

"Sit down. **I want to do the thing properly and I have written it out and I am going to read it out, and you are going to let me, and then you are going to say something, and I do not know what you are going to say and I have decided that I do not want to know until you do.**"

"Mara—"

"**No. Read the page first.**"

The page is dated the sixth of the sixth and it has two hands on it and no witness and there is one clause on it and she has read it to him about nine times.

> *Neither of us uses a result without naming its blind margin.*

"Right," he said. "**Go on.**"

"**Here is the blind margin and it took me three weeks to find it and it is the whole of what I have to say.** The figure is fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen. It is a sum of four hundred and eleven head counts and every head count is a number written on a form about a *class* — a standing withdrawal of the maintenance-deferred class, a series, a date, a price and a countersignature. **A standing withdrawal says what was withdrawn. It does not say who was in the class.**

"And here is what that means and it is five sentences and I have written them to go on the board on Monday morning under the working and not on a separate sheet, because a margin on a separate sheet is a margin nobody reads:

> ***One.*** **The audit cannot say where any of the fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen were. Not one street. Not one house. Residence is not on the form, and a form about a class has never in this city carried an address, and I have checked four hundred and eleven lines for an address and there is not one.**
> ***Two.*** **It cannot say one name.**
> ***Three.*** **It cannot say whether any of them is alive this afternoon. Every one of the four hundred and eleven lines is a statement about a class and not about a person, and a person who was taken out of a series in 1939 may or may not be alive and the series does not know and the series is not required to.**
> ***Four.*** **It cannot check the more than one half. The Registry's minute says more than one half are resident in this district and in the wards adjoining it. The working says one hundred and three of the four hundred and eleven are countersigned on behalf of a district having no standing under the water act. Those are different claims and the audit verifies neither of them, because where a person lived is not on the form.**
> ***Five, and this is the one that matters and it is the reason I have not slept properly for a month.*** **A person can be on two of those four hundred and eleven lines.**

She put the folder down.

"**A standing withdrawal has no re-instatement column on it. There is no column anywhere in this series for a class that was withdrawn and then put back. So a man who was counted in 1939 and counted again in 1946, or a district that was withdrawn four times in six years because a class was invented and abolished and invented again — and the class this series is made of was invented in 1931 and abolished in 1957, so a site could go through it more than once, and I have looked for the sites and I have found nine that appear twice and I have found them because a price and a date are the same on two lines and not because anybody recorded that they are the same site.**"

"And the margin on that."

"**Fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen is a sum of head counts. Head counts are not people. The number of people is lower than the number and this office cannot say by how much, and the difference is not a rounding error, and the only honest way to state it is that the difference is the number of times one person was taken out of a district's standing twice and counted for it twice at two shillings a head for a year both times.**

"**And here is the money version, and I have decided to state it as a margin on the board and not to audit it, and it is worse.** The price on every line is per head per year. The years are not the same years. A head withdrawn in the storm month and not put back has cost eighteen years of a price and a head withdrawn last month has cost a month of it. **Four hundred and eleven lines do not say which is which, because the form has no column for a re-instatement and no column for a duration.** So the money is a second audit, and the second audit is not this one, and it is not this week, and it will be done properly or not at all."

She looked at him.

"**That is the blind margin. It is five sentences, it has a working on it, and it is going on the same board as the figure on Monday morning, under it, in the Office's type, and the figure is not to be used by anybody who has not read it, and I am going to say so in the same sentence in which I say the figure is published, because that is the only instrument I have and it is a sentence.**"

---

"And then she said the other thing, and she had been carrying it for about three weeks and she gave it flatly, and she is a person who has been in a building for nine years and knows exactly how a sentence will be read."

"**You are going to ask me what it does to me and I am going to answer it before you ask, and the reason is that I have found out this month what I am, and I would rather say it here where there are three witnesses than have it said in a corridor.**

"I am a source. **That is the whole of what a figure with a working on it turns its maker into, and it happened on the Friday morning of the twenty-eighth of the tenth at about a quarter to eleven, in a public room, in front of nine people, and I did not notice for about four days.** Forty to sixty thousand on two boards was a courtesy. It could be quoted, argued with, and denied, and there was nothing in it that anybody had to be the author of. **A figure with a working on it can be quoted and cannot be argued with except by doing the working again, and the person who can be found holding it is the woman whose office has four staff and one window and a written right to be ignored.**

"**And in about four years a room will ask about the water in this district and it will hold up the first figure anybody in this city ever published about it, and it will be in my office's type, and the office is me, and I will be the woman who counted it.** Every objection that anybody makes to that figure from now on will be an objection to me and not to the arithmetic, and the arithmetic is the only part of it that cannot be harmed, and the other part is a woman of thirty-three who has been the only person who works in an office of four staff for four years and who on the twentieth of the ninth told a man in a shop that a drawer holds a card about nine hundred times.

"**That is what a figure does to the person who makes it. It makes her a drawer with a number on the front of it.** And I have not put it in a drawer. It is on a board. That is the whole difference and it took me four years and a Tuesday afternoon to be able to say that both of those sentences are true at once, and I am not going to be thanked, and I have told two people in this building already and I am telling you because you are the only other person alive who has to carry a version of it."

---

**And then he did not say it, and the not saying is the last third of the afternoon and it is not a conversation and it is not a silence about the audit.**

He has known since the twenty-fifth of the ninth that the ward's reserve is under a day. He has known since the second of the third that the low-level alarm is inhibited, and he has known since the seventh of October that it is at about fourteen hours and exactly balanced and that no margin has been in it since March, and he has known since the eighth of October that a man of forty-one at a works' gate has been writing that number on a sheet of paper and folding it into his coat, once a day, since the second of the ninth and for about eleven weeks, because a man can wash his hands.

He has told nobody any of it. **There is no form on which to tell anybody any of it.** The warden's day book is in a box in a store and the person who kept it is on a bus.

And on the Tuesday afternoon, at about four o'clock, in a room with three people in it, a woman of thirty-three who audits tolls said five sentences about a blind margin, and he agreed with every one of them, and he had a second fact sitting in him the whole time and he did not say it, and he had a reason ready, and the reason is in his own book from the sixth of June and it is a good one: **the moment she has it she will put it in a drawer, and the drawer is the thing she told him about in September, and the Office of Public Reckoning is the one instrument in this city that counts anything, and he would rather be the only person in Morrowfen holding a number about a reserve that is at fourteen hours than be the man who handed it to the one office that could not be got to act on it and could not be got to refuse it either.**

And that arrangement is the one he wrote down in the grey book in June and called a coward's arrangement and said was the best one available to him.

She noticed.

She did it the way she does everything, by reading a room, and she has been reading rooms professionally for nine years, and it took her about four minutes, and she did not look up, and she said:

"**You have had something since September.**"

"Yes."

"**I am not asking.**" She put her hand flat on the table, which is a thing she does. "**And I am going to tell you why I am not asking, and then I am going to stop, and we are going to go on with the page exactly as it is.**

"The clause does not say that we tell each other everything. I worked that out in September in about a minute and a half and I have not looked at it since and it is still right. It says we do not use a result without naming its margin. **It has nothing to say about what we keep, and I spent four months believing that a man who observes his own blind margins is a man with no blind margins worth naming, and he had two since the fourth of the fourth, and I sat across a table from him.**

"**So I am not asking, and you are not going to tell me, and the two facts are the same shape as the thing we wrote down in the room over a chandler's shop on the fourteenth of the tenth, and we did not go and knock on twenty-six doors, and we wrote four lines about it and named the price, and nobody has thanked anybody and nobody was going to.**"

"I am going to be asked to stand on a street," he said, "on the tenth of the twelfth, and say three hundred minutes out loud, and I am not going to be able to refuse it."

"Yes."

"And you are going to be asked to publish a number that is fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen with a margin on it, and somebody in a room in about four years is going to be angry with you rather than with it."

"**Yes,**" said Mara Venn, "and that is the correct arrangement and it is the one I would have chosen and I am not going to pretend that it is a comfort, and it is not a comfort, and it is the first time in nine years that I have put a number where somebody was going to be angry at it, and I have put five sentences under it so that the anger has to be about the right thing, **and if I had put it in a drawer I would have been able to sleep and I would have been the reason nobody ever saw it.**"

---

**And then she took the decision, and it was hers, and it was not his to take, and he was in the room, and he did not get to decide it.**

"Right," she said. "**The audit is for the return.**"

"What?"

"**The district's return is nil and it has been nil since the storm month, and a nil return is a statement that the thing is not in the schedule at all, and a district that is not in the schedule is a district whose water nobody is billed for, and this ward has been carrying the water of a hundred and forty thousand people since 1936. The figure of fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen does not change the rate by a penny and it is not going to. What changes the rate is the return, and the return is corrected by entering the district's hours, and entering the district's hours raises it, and raising it is the price of the water.**

"**And that is the first thing anybody in this city has ever handed me that is a decision and not a discovery, and I am taking it, and I am not asking you, and you do not get a vote.**

"The audit is entered on the ward's return as a schedule, with the working and the five lines of margin, and the return goes to the rate authority before the rate is struck, and the ward's rate goes up, and the increase falls on about two hundred and eleven households in nine blocks of a landlord's and a rateable figure in a street that is not in the district. **And the people who will be angry about it in about four years will be the ones who live on that street, and they will be right to be, and the number that made them angry is the same number that is on a board in Cathedral Rise with five sentences under it, and both of those are mine now.**"

"You are going to do it because the count is the only thing anybody can check," he said.

"**I am going to do it because a range is a thing a room can be told and a figure is a thing a room can be given, and a room can be given one, and the giving changes what the room owes anybody, and I have been in nine rooms this year and I have got nothing on paper except a certificate in a glass case and six figures in chalk and a card in a nurses' room, and I would not have had one of them without a man who could be asked to explain it.**" She shut the folder. "**And I am not going to be thanked, and I am not going to be defended, and if the rate goes up in the twelfth month I am going to be the name on the schedule and not the Office's, and I am not going to hide behind four staff and a window.**"

She put her hand off the table and picked up her bag, and at the door she stopped and did not turn round.

"**Whatever the second fact is: it is yours, and I have not asked, and I will use a figure of mine on Monday and not use a fact of yours at all, and that is the only way either of us gets to be trusted by somebody on the tenth of the twelfth, and I would like it noticed that I have worked that out and it took me about four minutes and it cost me the thing I have wanted all year.**"

And she went out into the court, and there were three desks in it and two of them were occupied, and the Office has one window and a written right to be ignored and a drawer of four-line cards that held forty-six in September and has not been counted since, and nobody in this city can say what is in it, and that is the correct condition of it, and she said so in September, and she has not said so since because she has said it once and once is enough.

**Nobody was thanked. The clerk at the licensing floor, who has four years in and has not been thanked for anything, was not thanked. The two of them have a page between them that is dated and has two hands on it and no witness, and neither of them has called it a friendship and neither of them is going to today, and on the sixth of the sixth, in a public room, a woman of thirty-three put five sentences and a working on a wall under a figure, and the fifth sentence is that a person can be in two of the four hundred and eleven lines, and it is the most useful sentence anybody has said in this district this year, and it is true, and it is not comfortable, and it is on a wall where anybody can read it, and it was written by the person who made the figure and not by the person who owns the money.**
