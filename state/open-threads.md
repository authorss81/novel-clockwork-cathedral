Threads are opened here, carried in the volume that owns them, and closed in the volume named below. Do not open a new mystery in a volume to replace one that is already scheduled to close.

## Central mystery, in three layers

1. **Surface:** why does the Cathedral's public clock tick a ninth time, and why does a correct Reading leave a district worse off?
2. **Historical:** what happened during the Long Nine, who authorized the Censor Gate, and what did Edwin Vale do when he opened the bypass?
3. **Structural:** what is the Dark Reservoir, and why does the machine grow more willing to remove dissent as it grows more accurate?

## Intentionally unresolved, and not to be "fixed"

- The original Cooperative engineers' private reasoning.
- The lived intervals taken by severe body tolls. Some families know; some never will.
- Whether the First Hour had anything like awareness. The book confirms a rule and a learned threshold, not a mind. A character may use the word *remember*; the narration may not.
- How much uncertainty Morrowfen can afford. That is a continuing civic question, not a dangling supernatural thread.
