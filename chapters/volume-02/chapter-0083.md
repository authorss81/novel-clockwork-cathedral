# Chapter 83 — An Audit Is a Thing That Is Done

The Office of Public Reckoning put in a paper on Tuesday the twenty-fifth of the tenth month and paid three shillings, and it was told on the Thursday that the box may not leave the building, which was true and which nobody had suggested, and on the Friday morning two auditors and a clerk of that office sat down in a public reading room on Cathedral Rise in front of nine people and added up four hundred and eleven numbers in the order they are printed, and the answer is fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen, and it has a working on it, and the working is published, and the number is entered as received and noted, and it is Mara Venn's, and she is not going to be thanked for it.

---

**Tuesday the twenty-fifth, about a quarter past eleven in the morning, at the one window of the Office of Public Reckoning, in a building with a court outside the window.**

The form is four lines long and the fee is on it.

> *That the Office of Public Reckoning, being the office to which the second set of columns of the public toll ledger is a standing requirement, be permitted to inspect the batch register of the Registry of Transfers and Access, being a Directorate record in a box, for the purpose of an audit of the transfer and access series, and to take away such working as the audit produces.*
> *Fee: three shillings. **Not to be removed from the building.***

"That last line," said the man in the chair, without looking up. "That is not on the form."

"**No. It is mine. I have written it on there myself in a hand you can see is not the hand of the printer, and if you refuse the form you refuse the form with my sentence on it, and I would like you to refuse it in writing, and I would like the refusal to be dated, because a thing I have been told in a room is a thing I have been told in a room.**"

"Three shillings is the fee for a copy and this is not a copy."

"Then take the three shillings for the copy of the fee receipt and let the audit be done in the room," said Mara Venn. "**The fee is not the obstacle and you and I both know what the obstacle is and it is upstairs and it is not a person.**"

---

**Thursday the twenty-seventh, a reply, in four lines, in the Registry's type, and it is the first thing anybody in this city has received from that office that reads as though it was written by somebody who has read the request.**

> *1. The batch register is a Directorate record and is not, and has not been since about 1920, carried out of the room in which it is kept.*
> *2. The **index** of batches is a public book on a public table in this city and has been since the fifteenth of the ninth, and anybody may read it without fee and without leave, and twenty-six people did so on the Saturday and four on the Sunday and the keeper opened a door for the four.*
> *3. The Office of Public Reckoning may audit the index, in this room, at the table, on three working days, between ten and one, in the presence of the keeper, with such working as the audit produces, and such working is made for the Office and does not leave the room.*
> *4. **The register itself is not produced, and this office has no power to produce it and no form on which it could be required to.***

And then, because somebody in that Registry is paid to think about the fourth question:

> *The Office will observe that what it has asked for is not the register. It has asked for four hundred and eleven numbers, and four hundred and eleven numbers are in a public room and cost nothing. **We do not know why anybody would pay three shillings for a room when the numbers are on a table for nothing, and we record that we do not know, because this office has been wrong in public twice and prefers the record of it to the explanation of it.***

---

**Friday the twenty-eighth and Saturday the twenty-ninth of the tenth, from ten until about one, in the reading room, and the number of people in it went from two to nine and back to four and nobody was asked anything.**

Two of them are of the Office. The Office has four staff and one window and a written right to be ignored, and it had never in nine years had a second person in a room with it, and on Friday morning it had two, and one of the two was the man who had said *no, it is a procedure* in a room on the seventeenth of September, and he was right then and he was right now and neither of them said so.

The keeper stood at the end of the table with his hands behind his back and read the book over their shoulders and said nothing, because it is his table and a person having written something down at it is doing what it is for, and because **an index entry is a statement that something was taken out; it is not the thing that was taken out, and the day an index entry becomes a copy of what it indexes is the day somebody changes a head count in a room with no light in it**, and neither of those two auditors asked for a single sheet to be taken out of the room, and that is the reason he did not have to say either sentence out loud.

**The working.** It is four sheets of the Office's own foolscap and it is the whole of the audit and it is the reason the number can be checked by anybody:

> *AUDIT OF THE TRANSFER AND ACCESS SERIES, being the sum of the head counts entered in the index of batches of the Registry of Transfers and Access, in the order printed, in three sittings.*
>
> *411 lines. Columns of ten, added down and then across, and each column total carried to the foot and the foot added separately, and the two foot totals compared before publication. **They agree.***
> *Range published on the fifteenth of the ninth: not less than 40,000 and not more than 60,000. **This audit does not multiply that range and no person in this room has done so, and the operation is not the same operation: a range is one number with an uncertainty on it and an audit is four hundred and eleven numbers added. I have been told twice this year that a figure in a newspaper has a source, and this is the source, and it is a printed book anybody may go and read, and the sum of it can be done again by anybody with a pencil and about a fortnight.***
>
> ***SUM OF HEAD COUNTS: 51,418.***
>
> *Of the 411 lines, **103** are countersigned on behalf of a district having no standing under the water act. **The rule is that every fourth of the four hundred and eleven is countersigned on behalf of a district having no standing, and four hundred and eleven is not divisible by four and comes to a hundred and two and three-quarters. The rule is a rule of thumb. It is in no form and it is printed nowhere and it is quoted in four rooms because a man said it once in a yard. It does not hold exactly and the Office checked it twice and the figure is one hundred and three and not one hundred and two and not one hundred and four.**
> *Of the 411 lines, the prices run from 1s 4d to 2s the head per year. **No line carries a price that is not a price.***
>
> ***RECEIVED AND NOTED. Published with the working on it, in the form in which the working was made, with the number of lines and not with a copy of any of them.***

And it was published on the Saturday morning, on a board outside the room, in the Office's type, and the Office has one window and a written right to be ignored and printed it anyway, and a woman of thirty-three stood beside it for about a quarter of an hour and did not explain it to anybody, because a figure that is explained is a figure somebody has been given an opinion about.

---

**And on the Saturday afternoon at about twenty past two, inside a minute and a half of each other, two people used it, and nobody afterwards was able to say which was first and neither of them tried.**

The first was on the telephone, and the Registry's clerk took the call in the corridor outside the reading room and wrote it on a pad, and it was **Perry Shaw, 47, Commissioner of the Registry of Transfers and Access**, and he said one sentence and hung up.

"**Then I was right, and I am entitled to say so, and I am going to say so once, on the record, in a room with a clerk in it, and then I am not going to say it again: a number in a set of minutes whose source is a box may be called a fabrication, and it has not been called one, and the figure that has come back is inside the range I published, and I have said all along that a Registry can stand behind a range in a morning and cannot stand behind a figure, and today a figure has come back with a working on it out of a public room, and I am glad of it, and it is the last time I shall say so.**"

The second was a woman of fifty-four with nine blocks and about two hundred and eleven households, who came in off Bellweather Road at about two o'clock with her hat still on and put a card on the table, and the card is the printed card the Office had nailed to the board, and the figures on it are in the Office's type.

"The figure is fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen people," said Hanna Stroud, "and I have read the working and I have done the sum twice with a pencil at the counter of my own bank on a Saturday morning, and it is a figure and not a range, and **I am going to use it, and I am going to use it against myself, and I would like everybody in this room to understand that before I use it.**

"Every one of those forms was a return. **A return is what a building is instead of a person, and a form is what a class is instead of a person, and every single price on every one of those four hundred and eleven lines is a decision about somebody's standing, made by a person, at a price, on a form, in daylight, correctly.** And I have read the working, and the working cannot tell me one name, and it cannot tell me one street, and it cannot tell me whether any of the fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen people is alive this afternoon, and **that is not a flaw in the audit. That is the audit.** The blind margin of four hundred and eleven head counts is that it is a count of heads and a head is not a person and I have known that since I was thirty and I have been charging rent on it for twenty-two years.

"**So here is what I am going to do with it, on the record, and I am not asking anybody in this room for anything.** The district's return is nil. It has been nil since the storm month. **A nil return is a statement that the thing is not in the schedule at all, and a district that is not in the schedule is a district whose water is nobody's bill, and a district whose water is nobody's bill is a district that gets the whole of it, and I have been paying that since 1936 out of rents from two hundred and eleven households.** Correcting the return raises the rate. **Correcting the return is the only thing I have ever found in this city that costs a district money on purpose and in writing and in a form anybody can be refused in.** I am going to ask for the return to be corrected, in the name of nine blocks, on a stated day, with this figure on the table, and it will raise the bill, and I am not going to be a woman who wanted a number and got a feeling, and I am going to be a woman who wanted a number and got a number and is going to pay for it."

She put the card down flat on the table, which is a thing three people in this book do.

"And that is my use of it and I am not asking whether it is the right one, because the Commissioner had his use first and I do not know when it was and I do not think anybody in this building does, **and I am not going to stand here and tell a room of eleven people that my use of a number is better than a Commissioner's use of the same number, because that is exactly what a person does when there is a figure and no way to argue with it.**"

---

**And the finding is in the pumping ward's outer room on the Saturday evening at about six, and Bevin Sarr wrote it on the wall in chalk because that is what the wall is for, and she wrote the working on it as well because she has learned what a working is this year.**

> *Saturday the twenty-ninth of the tenth. **51,418.** Four hundred and eleven head counts added in the order printed, three sittings, in the reading room at the Archive, with a working on it, published with the working on it, entered as received and noted. **It is not mine. It is the Office of Public Reckoning's and the woman who did it is not going to be thanked and has said so in a corridor.*
> *The range on two boards is 40,000 to 60,000. **The figure is inside the range. That is the whole of what the figure does to the range and it does not kill it and it does not replace it: a range is what an office can say in a morning without a pencil, and a figure is what an office can be asked to defend, and this city has had one of those for eighteen years and has never had the other.*
> *The blind margin, and it is named: **the audit cannot say where any of the fifty-one thousand four hundred and eighteen were. It cannot say one street. It cannot say one name. It cannot say whether any of them is alive. It cannot check the more than half, because residence is not on the form, and a form about a class has never in this city carried an address.*
> *And a figure with a working on it is a thing a room can be given. **A range is a thing a room can be told. That is the difference and it took a fortnight and a tin at a bank counter and a woman with nine blocks, and nobody thanked her either.***

Nobody thanked anybody. The Commissioner said his piece once and hung up. The woman paid for her own use in advance and in public. The keeper stood at the end of his own table with his hands behind his back and read four hundred and eleven numbers for six hours and has never been thanked for anything and is sixty-one.
