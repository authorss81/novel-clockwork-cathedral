## Fixed figures used (from VOLUME-03-BRIEF §7, not re-derived)

- Night of drift 50 seconds. 100 minutes = 6,000 s = 120 nights = 4 months.
- Rehearsal 17th twelfth figures + 50 s/night; separations 9 min 20 s and 51 min 50 s do not move. To 13th first = 27 nights = 22 min 30 s (used as stated, not recomputed on page).
- Hospital branch 4th eleventh 2 h 01 min 00 s + 76 nights (1 h 03 min 20 s) = 3 h 04 min 20 s on 19th first, gaining 50 s/night.
- Token price 4s 6d (54d). Night at price about halfpenny. Year on one clock 5 h 04 min 10 s. Three tokens 13s 6d. Ward slack 40 minutes; 100 minutes = 2.5× slack; two tokens 9s, ward has no fund.
- Supply (hall's own numbers, on a plate, said by Hale 17th): 261 cut last return, 206 taken back, 940 sold, 516 unaccounted for.
- Assay: beam, standard weights to half grain, free, about 4 minutes. Keeper weighed nine in nineteen years, all brought back. Real 1,860 grains; false 1,700 (book figure, none seen).
- Rate: ward's rate 3s in pound on £6,960 = £1,044 a year, Mara's name on schedule. Office figure 51,418 used as Office figure only, no percentage or ratio, no other total. Range never stated or multiplied.
